## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an immersive, high-stakes scholarly environment blending arcane Hextech fantasy with rigorous pharmacological science. The visual language evokes a Piltover-meets-Zaun high-tier academy laboratory: precision crystal glasswares, ornate runic chassis, polished brass gears, and pulsating alchemical reagents.

### Aesthetic Foundation
- **Style Archetype:** Arcane Neoclassical & High-Fantasy Art Deco. It integrates ornate metallic filigree with surgical scientific UI structures.
- **Atmosphere:** Scholarly, mystical, and razor-sharp. Deep sub-oceanic and void navy backdrops contrast sharply against polished gilded accents, brass bevels, and hyper-luminous mana energy conduits.
- **Target Audience:** Medical practitioners, pharmacology scholars, and interactive learners who demand deep anatomical, biochemical, and clinical precision framed through an evocative fantasy narrative.
- **Emotional Response:** Inspires mastery, calculated discovery, and elite craftsmanship. The interface feels valuable, dangerous, and meticulously engineered.

## Colors

The color system operates on an alchemical balance: cold, dense arcane voids illuminated by high-karat gold metalwork, luminous cyan mana reactors, and vivid reagent indicators.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Metal (`#C8AA6E` - Polished Hextech Gold):** Applied to structural chassis, runic filigree, focus indicators, active tabs, and primary action boundaries. Highlights scale up to `#F0E6D2` (Sunken Parchment Gold) with deep shadows sinking into `#785A28` (Aged Brass).
- **Secondary Arcane (`#0AC8B9` - Luminous Mana Cyan):** Used for synthetic mana conduits, telemetry values, chemical ionization states, interactive active-glow states, and kinetic energy graphs. Transition shades extend down to `#005A82` for submerged conduit lines.
- **Tertiary Reagents (`#E84057` - Crimson Elixir):** Reserved for acute contraindications, toxicity warnings, receptor antagonism, and volatile drug interactions. Paired alongside an auxiliary alchemical emerald (`#00A86B`) for bio-affinity, receptor agonism, and therapeutic windows.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#071224` - Abyssal Navy):** Foundation surfaces utilize dark layers starting at `#040814` (Deep Core Void) stepping up through `#071224` (Base Canvas) and `#0B1A30` (Apothecary Slate).

### Functional Color Rules
- Never use pure white (`#FFFFFF`) for typography or borders; use `#F0E6D2` to maintain the warm, gilded illumination of ambient mana crystals.
- Backgrounds must remain anchored in desaturated, high-depth navy to ensure cyan and gold elements emit stark, controlled luminance without visual fatigue.

## Typography

The typographic balance bridges monumental classical scholarship with clinical laboratory readability. 

- **Display & Headlines (`EB Garamond`):** Evokes ancient medical treatises, Trajan lapidary craftsmanship, and classical academy prestige. All headline styles leverage slight tracking and dignified weight to project authority. Set larger titles in uppercase or small-caps where appropriate for ritualistic cataloging.
- **Clinical Body (`Hanken Grotesk`):** Provides razor-sharp, modern typographic rhythm for complex biochemical pathways, drug monographs, dosage equations, and multi-paragraph diagnostic text. High x-height ensures clarity under low-light UI conditions.
- **Instrument Labels & Telemetry (`Space Grotesk`):** Delivers geometric, technical precision for pharmacokinetic measurements, half-life statistics, molar weights, enzyme binding affinities, and rune-coded system indices. Always uppercase with increased letter spacing.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts conform to a structured, symmetrical 12-column grid system reminiscent of high-fantasy blueprint schematics and alchemical drafting tables.

### Layout Philosophy
- **Modular Drafting System:** Surfaces are segregated into distinct apparatus modules—Specimen Chambers, Formulation Slates, and Chemical Reaction Sequences.
- **Breakpoints:**
  - **Desktop (1280px and above):** 12-column fluid-locked grid with `margin-lg` (3rem) and `gutter-lg` (2rem). Content width caps at 1440px to preserve drafting table proportionality.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column grid with `margin` (2rem) and `gutter` (1.5rem). Secondary apothecary shelves collapse into retractable slide-over panels.
  - **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4-column layout with `margin-sm` (1rem) and `gutter-sm` (0.75rem). Dense technical cards stack vertically; potion synthesis matrices adapt to scrollable sequential carousels.

### Component Spacing Rhythm
- Density is calibrated for dense data consumption. Utilize `space-sm` (8px) for internal input and chip padding, `space-md` (16px) between related data points, and `space-xl` (40px) to distinguish isolated alchemical apparatus cards.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed through physical layers of crystal glass, metallic chassis frames, and multi-spectral mana luminescence rather than standard drop shadows.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (The Abyssal Table):** Deep `#040814` to `#071224` matte background. Zero emission, minimal ambient light absorption.
- **Level 1 (Apparatus Chassis):** Solid `#0B1A30` slate bordered with muted antique brass (`#785A28`, 1px solid). Emits subtle dark-blue occlusion (`0 8px 24px rgba(4, 8, 20, 0.7)`).
- **Level 2 (Crystal Glass Panels):** Translucent mana glass overlaying active synthesizers (`rgba(11, 26, 48, 0.75)` backdrop blur 12px) framed by dual-tone metallic gold trims (`1px solid #C8AA6E33`) with directional specular highlights at top-left vertices.
- **Level 3 (Mana-Charged Interactive State):** Activated components project an internal cyan aura (`inset 0 0 12px rgba(10, 200, 185, 0.25)`) and external hextech projection casting (`0 0 20px rgba(10, 200, 185, 0.35)`).
- **Level 4 (Alchemical Modals & Tooltip Prisms):** Elevated diagnostic views with a reinforced brass structural rim (`2px solid #C8AA6E`) flanked by rune corner brackets and deep drop shadows (`0 16px 40px rgba(2, 6, 14, 0.9)`).

## Shapes

The interface rejects soft, organic consumer curvatures in favor of architectural, precision-machined geometry inspired by crystal facets, art-deco brackets, and hextech runestones.

### Geometric Rules
- **Base Curvature:** Set to `0` (Sharp). UI elements maintain crisp, hard 90-degree junctions or 45-degree chamfers.
- **Chamfered Edges:** Core interactive elements (primary buttons, major card banners, diagnostic status containers) utilize 4px to 8px angled cut corners (`clip-path: polygon(...)`), simulating carved crystal blocks and reinforced metal plating.
- **Filigree Brackets:** Modals and focal cards feature custom 4-corner brass brackets that project outward 2px beyond the component boundary.
- **Conduit Dividers:** Separators are styled as fine, dual-groove brass lines featuring a centered diamond or runic node ornament.

## Components

### Buttons & Actuators
- **Primary Alchemical Action:** Chamfered corners (6px cut), dark navy base (`#071224`), surrounded by a 1px solid polished gold border (`#C8AA6E`). Typography uses `label-lg` in `#F0E6D2`. Hover induces a cyan interior glow (`inset 0 0 14px rgba(10, 200, 185, 0.3)`) and gold border luminescence (`0 0 10px rgba(200, 170, 110, 0.5)`).
- **Secondary / Utility Button:** Flat `#0B1A30` plate with a sharp rectangular profile, bordered in muted brass (`#785A28`). Text is `#C8AA6E`. Hover lightens the slate background by 10%.
- **Ghost / Runemark Button:** Unbordered, transparent background with `label-md` uppercase text in `#0AC8B9`. Underlined by an animated 1px cyan ray that expands outward from center on hover.

### Chemical Chips & Pharmacological Tags
- **Receptor Chips:** Compact (height: 24px), sharp edges, dark navy interior (`#040814`) with a 1px border corresponding to biological polarity:
  - *Agonist / Binding:* Emerald (`#00A86B`) border with glowing indicator dot.
  - *Antagonist / Inhibitor:* Crimson (`#E84057`) border with pulsing aura.
  - *Allosteric Modulator:* Arcane Cyan (`#0AC8B9`) border.
- **Font Styling:** `label-sm` Space Grotesk, uppercase with high letter-spacing.

### Inputs & Dosage Adjusters
- **Text & Formula Fields:** Recessed field with solid `#040814` canvas, 1px bottom border in `#785A28`. When focused, bottom border transitions to `#C8AA6E` with an upward vertical cyan gradient wash (`linear-gradient(0deg, rgba(10, 200, 185, 0.08) 0%, transparent 100%)`).
- **Runic Sliders & Dials:** Track styled as an illuminated conduit line with a solid brass diamond slider thumb that emits a subtle cyan beacon when dragged.

### Checkboxes & Segmented Selectors
- **Checkboxes:** 16x16px diamond-oriented square (`transform: rotate(45deg)`). Unchecked state shows an antique brass wireframe. Checked state fills with `#0AC8B9` and displays a high-contrast dark center pip.
- **Radio Buttons:** Concentric dual-ring brass chassis. Active selection illuminates an inner solid `#C8AA6E` core.

### Apothecary Cards & Specimen Slates
- **Anatomy:** 
  1. *Outer Shell:* High-density `#0B1A30` slate with a 1px continuous border (`#C8AA6E33`).
  2. *Header Plate:* Inset parchment or etched glass bar featuring `headline-md` Garamond typography paired with gold badge brackets.
  3. *Core Body:* Frosted pane presenting compound structures, pharmacokinetics charts, or titration curves.
  4. *Footer:* Telemetry data stream rendered in `label-sm` Space Grotesk.

### Specialized Academy Components
- **Mechanism-of-Action Pathway Visualizer:** Interactive biochemical flowchart where nodes are styled as glowing runic crystals interconnected by animated mana pulse conduits.
- **Vial & Reagent Gauge:** Vertical cylindrical indicator showing drug serum levels, therapeutic windows (safe zone indicated by gold markers), and toxic thresholds (marked by crimson warning lines).
- **Runic Inscription Tooltips:** Micro-inspection panels enclosed in crystal glass with corner rivets, surfacing molecular weight, clearance rate, and enzyme affinities on node hover.
